Compiled truth: Beanstalk Roasters is a small-batch wholesale coffee roaster supplying [[people/mara-okafor]] with the house blend whole bean since opening. On 2026-02-22 Beanstalk announced a 22% wholesale price increase effective 2026-03-01, raising the unit price to $915.
