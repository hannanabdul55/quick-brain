Spike 002 · Accounting API Head-to-Head

QBO (the v1.1 Phase 6 commitment) vs Xero, Wave, and FreshBooks across 5 axes. Worked against Mara's Coffee persona (~10 employees, US-based, ~$500k revenue, 5-10 vendors).

QuickBooks Online

KEEP for v1.1

Best raw throughput, longest refresh tokens, dominant US market. Community-maintained TS SDK is the friction.

Xero

ADD as 2nd connector in v1.2

Best dev velocity (Demo Company, native TS SDK, single base URL). Tighter rate limit (60/min) but acceptable for Mara-sized accounts.

Wave

DO NOT pursue

No new dev applications accepted since 2024. Free-text vendor model would be a transformer mess anyway.

FreshBooks

DEFER — freelancer SKU only

12-hour refresh tokens kill scheduled syncs. Time Tracking + OCR matter for a solo-operator product, not for Mara's persona.

Recommendation

v1.1 Phase 6: stick with QBO. Highest US market share, best raw throughput (8× Xero's rate limit), longest refresh tokens. The "harder dev experience" axes (separate sandbox URL, community-maintained TS SDK) cost ~1-2 hours of one-time setup — small against the persona-fit win.

v1.2+ "Multi-Connector": add Xero as the second connector. Comparable persona fit (Xero owns AU/NZ/UK SMB market) and materially better dev velocity. Designing the v1.2 connector trait against both QBO and Xero from day one is the right architectural call — their data shapes are close enough to share a transformer interface.

Skip Wave and FreshBooks for v1.x. Wave is access-blocked and structurally wrong (no Bill entity). FreshBooks targets a different persona (freelancers) and the 12h refresh-token policy fights any scheduled-sync use case from Spike 001.

Implications for the v1.1 Phase 6 plan

  • No change to current Phase 6 scope. Continue with QBO as the single connector.
  • When writing the lib/qbo/transformer.ts, name the connector-agnostic types (Bill, Vendor, BankLine) so a Xero adapter can target the same interface in v1.2 without retro-fit.
  • Spike 001's vendor-email path lands on Vendor.PrimaryEmailAddr.Address — that field's existence is confirmed in QBO and Xero, absent in Wave and FreshBooks. Future-proofs the manual-trigger compose path.
  • Roadmap candidate: v1.2 "Multi-Connector" milestone — Xero ingest + connector-abstraction refactor + Phase-6 transformer split into lib/connectors/{qbo,xero}/transformer.ts with a shared lib/connectors/types.ts interface.
