<objective>
Remove the hardcoded FIXTURES_ROOT from lib/insights/cache.ts::computeAndCache
and make every call site pass a sourceDir explicitly. This fixes the critical bug
(Pitfall 1) where every tenant sees Mara's insight data instead of their own.

Purpose: Without this refactor, even if the smb-audit skill writes correct
concept pages to brains/<tenantId>/brain-repo/, the insight parser still reads
from data/maras-coffee/ and the skill output never reaches the dashboard.

Output: Three files modified. No new files created. Zero changes to the parser
logic or AnomalyRow types.
</objective>
