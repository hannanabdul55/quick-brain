<objective>
Implement the 30–45s narrated SSE onboarding stream that fires while the client waits for its dashboard. The stream emits 5 named stage frames at the timings locked in CONTEXT.md (5s + 12s + 10s + 8s + 1s = 36s), and during stage 3 ("building-graph") spawns a real `gbrain query --no-expand "Top vendors by total spend?"` subprocess against the new tenant's brain. The query result is discarded — its purpose is to warm PGLite buffers and the Anthropic prompt cache so the chat surface (Plan 06) feels faster.

Purpose: This is the half of onboarding that satisfies the "60-second narrated theater" demo promise. Without it, ONBD-04 (5 stages), ONBD-05 (real warm-up), and ONBD-07 (30–60s total wall-clock) all fail. The performance-critical decision (`--no-expand`) sidesteps the Phase 1 gotcha where `gbrain query` with expansion takes 3+ minutes.

Output: A typed SSE stream, a testable orchestrator function, and a Route Handler that wires them together using the Next.js 15 `ReadableStream` pattern.
</objective>
